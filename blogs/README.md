## What makes a draft ready

- Maintainer assignment and agreed scope are recorded.
- The implemented connector has been tested against a real instance.
- Exact versions, commands and actual retrieval results are included.
- Failure cases and limitations are explained from observed evidence.
- Shubhi has reviewed the code, technical claims and final wording herself.
- The PR and final blog URLs are recorded for submission through WeMakeDevs.
