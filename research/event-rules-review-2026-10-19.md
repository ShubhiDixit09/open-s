# Mergetober event-rule review

Notes from the full event-page review on 19 October 2026. The rules linked below remain authoritative. The repository's actual progress was checked again on 20 October.

## Before implementation

- Get actual maintainer assignment before writing connector code or opening a PR. A proposal, a request comment or a welcome bot is not assignment.
- Explain the approach and validation plan in an assignment request tagging the designated maintainer. Existing requests are linked in the [proposal index](../proposals/README.md); do not repeat them automatically.
- Five new proposals are permitted, but that does not establish a safe allowance of five simultaneous assignment requests. Only request work that is about to begin. The site does not publish a numeric safe limit for pending assignments.
- All five new-issue slots have been used. All five requests remain pending at the 20 October checkpoint. No code or prototypes have been written.

Source: [event rules, especially 4–8 and 10](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/mergetober/rules).

## What a submission needs

- A useful, working integration with clear setup, a runnable example, tests and validation against a real account or instance.
- One original real-use public blog per PR. The five saved articles remain AI-assisted working drafts; actual usage evidence and Shubhi's own review are still required.
- A PR opened during 1–31 October 2026. A later merge is allowed, but prize judging still requires a merged eligible PR submitted through the form.
- Both the PR and blog links submitted through the WeMakeDevs form when it becomes available. At review time, the page said the form was opening soon. The published page did not specify the deadline timezone.

Sources: [challenge, submission and FAQs](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/mergetober), [rules](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/mergetober/rules).

## Repository and authorship requirements

Use the appropriate Cognee repository/fork for upstream contributions. Data-source connectors belong in cognee-community, branching from main; core changes branch from dev. Follow the actual repository layout, contribution guide, examples and checks. Sign off every contribution commit and include the required DCO affirmation in the PR description.

The event is solo. AI can assist with learning, debugging and code, but the participant must understand, test and explain every submitted line. Original writing and accurate claims about completed work matter; making text sound less like AI is not a substitute for those requirements.

Sources: [builder's toolkit](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/mergetober/resources), [community contributing guide](https://github.com/topoteretes/cognee-community/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), [official contribution overview](https://docs.cognee.ai/contributing/contributing-overview).

## Eligibility, judging and conduct

The platform ordinarily permits one account per person and requires age 18 or parental/guardian consent. Account information must be accurate. Respect other contributors and maintainers; avoid pressure, harassment, plagiarism and spam.

Top Contributor is based on merged PRs submitted through the form, with quality and size deciding a tie. The random reward selects 20 people with an eligible merged submission; proposals are not raffle tickets. The top prize is a choice of the listed phone, laptop or $2,000 cash. Winners may need to provide delivery/payment details and handle applicable taxes or customs.

Sources: [terms](https://www.wemakedevs.org/terms), [Code of Conduct](https://www.wemakedevs.org/coc), [prizes and FAQs](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/mergetober#prizes).
